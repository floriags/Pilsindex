The BeerIndex (0–100) blends five signals from the forecast for each hour:

Temperature (35%) — peaks around 23–25°C, drops off toward cold or scorching extremes.
Wind (20%) — a light breeze (5–15 km/h) is ideal; dead calm is fine, gusty is not.
Humidity (15%) — most comfortable between 40–60%.
Cloud cover (10%) — some shade is nice, full overcast is a bit dull.
Rain chance — subtracted directly: a high chance of rain drags the score down hard, regardless of everything else.

Data from Open-Meteo (no key required). The first 48 hours are shown hour-by-hour; the following 5 days are grouped into 6-hour blocks, matching the forecast's native resolution.
